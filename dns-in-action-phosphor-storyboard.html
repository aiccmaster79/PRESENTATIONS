Facts and numbers this storyboard assumes

  • No SRT and no audio file were attached. The words and the times are a proposed voiceover at a teaching pace, with a pause of about half a second wherever the tube rolls to a new screen. Planned length is 61.50 seconds: the last word ends near 60.50, then one second on the takeaway.
  • The spoken script is condensed from the DNS in Action deck. It keeps the deck’s path (client, resolver, root, top-level domain, authoritative server), the deck’s tools (nslookup, ping by name, ping by IP, ping 8.8.8.8) and the deck’s lab warning. DHCP option 006, the hosts file, ipconfig /flushdns, and the career slides are in the deck and are left out of this cut.
  • www.example.com is the deck’s example name. A live lookup on 19 Oct 2026 returned two A records, 172.66.147.243 and 104.20.23.154, and two AAAA records, 2606:4700:10::6814:179a and 2606:4700:10::ac42:93f3. The apex A records had a TTL of 26 seconds; www.example.com’s A records had a TTL of 300 seconds. Nameservers were hera.ns.cloudflare.com and elliott.ns.cloudflare.com. There was no CNAME on www. These are Cloudflare anycast addresses and can change at any time. The glass shows the two IPv4 answers only. IPv6 is left off because the line does not say it. Re-check both A records on the day the animation is rendered.
  • Ping from this network to both A records, and to the name example.com, returned replies with no loss. The FAIL frame is the deck’s supervised lab test with a bad resolver. It is not a claim that www.example.com is down.
  • 8.8.8.8 is the public address the deck uses to show that IP connectivity and name resolution are different tests. On screen, “LINK UP” means that ping still gets an answer, which is the deck’s demonstration.
  • An A record is an IPv4 address and an AAAA record is an IPv6 address, as the deck’s lookup-path slide states. This cut speaks only the A record.
  • The bezel silk-screen reads NX-80 DNS TERMINAL. The reference machine said NEURAL TERMINAL; that name belongs to the other film. The phosphor pipeline, scanlines, barrel distortion, bloom, hum bar and glass are unchanged. The tiny silk-screen on the plastic (15px and 11px Space Mono) is part of that copied bezel. Story labels inside the tube are 28px or larger, headlines 44px or larger, and the key numbers 64px or larger.
  • Keyframes show the cursor lit. In the film it blinks. Each still is the finished hold at the end of its beat, so a label that arrives mid-sentence is already on the glass.
